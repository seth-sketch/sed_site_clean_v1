How we work

Built for air dates that don't move.

01 — Concept

Show format, camera plan and audience flow become sketches and full renderings for network sign-off.

02 — Drafting

Shop-ready plans, elevations and details on a consistent title-block standard that builders can bid from.

03 — Integration

LED, lighting and video coordinated early, with one-sheets that keep every vendor on the same pixel map.

04 — Build & air

On site through fabrication, load-in and rehearsals until the first show is on air.

Built in this studio

Forectra — the software we design and run shows with.

We got tired of stitching together drafting, LED, rigging and lighting tools, so we built our own. Forectra draws the set, the screens and the rig in your browser, then runs lights and video from the FOR-X 8 console. It runs Front Office Sports Tonight every night, broadcast on FOX and Samsung TV Plus.

The studio

A New York studio built on network television.

We bring together designers, drafters and lighting and LED specialists around every project, so the set, the screens and the light are worked out together from day one. Our work spans LED-forward studios for ABC News and ABC Daytime, the modular multi-show complex at Disney Hudson Square, election-night HQs, presidential debate stages, concerts and brand activations.

The studio is led by founder and Emmy-winning production designer Seth Easter, with more than two decades at ABC News and ABC Daytime and art direction on multiple Tony Awards broadcasts. We bring that network discipline to every client: broadcasters, agencies and brands who need a space that works on camera and in the room.
